PARADOX

It's funny how this was my word before it was ever ours. Before I even knew you, I was strangely obsessed with it. At first it felt confusing — almost heavy. Two opposites standing side by side. Sad — yet happy. Broken — yet beautiful. Losing... yet somehow still winning.

I used to think this word described me. How I could feel too much and yet say too little.

But then you happened.

And now when I think of you — when I think of us — this word comes back... softer this time. Because you are my paradox. You calm my chaos and somehow match it too. You understand my silence but laugh at my madness.

It's simple... just a word... but it holds so much depth.

And maybe that's what we are... not perfect.. not simple.. but something beautifully layered.

So if your mind ever feels heavy, if your thoughts ever get too loud, let me try to be the comfort to your chaos. Let me try to be the quiet in your storm. Let me try to be the place where your paradox feels safe.

Because somehow, my favorite word became my favorite person.

So maybe whenever things feel delicate, whenever we're low or don't know how to explain what we feel.. we'll just say it... Paradox.

And we'll understand.

And honestly? I kind of love that you're my favorite contradiction.

Our word. Always.

The Longest Night

When no one can tell you cried last night. Because you are the goated one. The strong one. The caretaker. The one who understands before people even speak. The listener. The guider. The teacher. The one giving everyone lessons about life while forgetting what life is doing to him.

You let them pour their souls into your hands. You collect their broken pieces. You tell them they will survive. You sit with their storms until the sky clears.

But why does the one who carries everyone never get to put his own weight down?

You think I opened up? You're delusional. If out of a hundred wounds you know one or two, that does not mean you know me. You only know the version of me that survived long enough to smile.

No one knows me.

I can read the silence in everyone's voice. I can hear the tears they never cried. At least let them cry in my arms. At least let them fall apart with me.

But where are the arms that will hold me until I stop pretending? Where is the place where I do not have to be strong? Where do I go when I am the home everyone else runs to? Who lets the shelter collapse?

Will someone ever let me cry? Really cry. Not the silent tears at three in the morning. Not the smile while my chest is bleeding. Not the "I'm fine" with a shaking voice.

Will I ever find my own arms? Will I ever find someone who understands without asking? Someone who notices the battle without seeing the scars? Someone who looks at me and says, "You don't have to carry the world today."

No. Because no one can be me. No one knows what it is like to drown while teaching others how to swim. No one knows what it is like to be everyone's safe place while having nowhere safe to go. No one knows what it is like to beg your own heart to survive one more night.

Maybe one day they'll realize. The strongest one in the room was never strong. He was just the one who cried so quietly, the world mistook his silence for strength.

It's me. Just me.

And the cruelest part? Even after all this — if you called me tonight and said, "I'm breaking, please stay" — I would still wipe my tears, hide my pain, and save you. While no one even notices that I was dying too.
