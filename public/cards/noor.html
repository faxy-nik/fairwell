Chapter 1 — A Random Group Chat

2023

We didn't meet through fate.

We met through chaos.

A random group chat from our old school. You were my junior who had already left. Just another person in a sea of messages.

Neither of us knew we'd still be talking years later.

🚫

Chapter 2 — "Main DM Mein Nahi Aati."

That sentence became your trademark.

Every time someone mentioned DMs, your answer was almost automatic.

"Sorry, main DM mein nahi aati."

Ironically...

The best part of our friendship started exactly there.

Chapter 3 — The DM Era

Somehow... you texted.

And everything clicked.

Dark humor. Unhinged jokes. Zero filters. Infinite sarcasm.

It felt less like talking to another person...

Even today, I still call you that.

My female variant.

Chapter 4 — The Friendship That Refused To End

Most friendships have one beginning.

Ours had several.

We got close. We drifted apart. Found each other again. Separated once more. Met again.

Some people leave once. We kept proving that our story wasn't finished.

🔥

Chapter 5 — October 2024

PGC Protest

The chapter that changed everything.

Out of all the chaos happening around us... our friendship found its strongest foundation.

Since that day, we've stayed.

Not because life became easier.

Because we finally chose to.

🌙

Chapter 6 — Your Break

There was a time when you disappeared.

You needed distance. I didn't stop you.

Truthfully... it hurt more than I admitted.

Sometimes caring means letting someone leave, even when every part of you wants them to stay.

Then one day... you came back. With regret. With honesty.

And somehow... our friendship became stronger than before.

🫂

Chapter 7 — The Battles Nobody Saw

You overthought everything. Sometimes your own thoughts became louder than reality.

You'd tell me about the strange paranormal things that scared you. Hospital visits because dehydration decided to become your monthly hobby. Days when life simply wasn't kind.

Your relationship with your mother wasn't always easy.

I couldn't fix those things. But I could stay. Sometimes that's enough.

🎥

Chapter 8 — The Reels

Thousands of chats. Random voice notes. Countless inside jokes.

Making reels together.

Turning ordinary days into memories we'll probably laugh about years from now.

💔

Chapter 9 — The People Who Left

There were chapters named Haris. There were chapters named Roohan.

People who once meant everything. Then... they became memories.

Life has a strange habit of introducing people just to test who remains.

When those chapters ended... I stayed. Not because I had to. Because I wanted to.

🤝

Chapter 10 — We Saved Each Other

People often think support only goes one way. It never did.

I stood beside you when your world felt heavy. You stood beside me when mine did.

You weren't just someone I helped. You helped me become someone stronger too.

For that... I'll always be grateful.

🎂

Chapter 11 — The Birthday Curse

There's one running joke between us.

It's funny now. Maybe one day we'll finally break that curse.

🌅

Three years. Several goodbyes. Several reunions. Hundreds of conversations. Thousands of laughs. More emotional talks than either of us will admit.

You're still the girl who overthinks. Still my female variant. Still the friend who somehow understands jokes nobody else would.

No matter how many times life separated us...
we always found our way back.

Maybe that's what real friendships are.
Not the ones that never break.
The ones that keep choosing each other after every crack.
