Before You. After You.

Some people don't leave memories. They leave a different version of you.

Before you...Days ended when the sun went down.
After you...They ended only after hearing your voice.
Before you...My phone was just a device.
After you...It became the place where my favorite person lived.
Before you...A notification was just a sound.
After you...One vibration could change my entire mood.
Before you...I looked at the sky because it was beautiful.
After you...I looked at it because I wondered if you were looking at the same one.
Before you...I remembered dates because they were on a calendar.
After you...I remembered them because they carried pieces of us.
Before you...Late nights felt lonely.
After you...Two in the morning became my favorite hour.
Before you...Silence felt awkward.
After you...Silence became another way of talking to you.
Before you...I thought love was something people confessed.
After you...I realized love is remembering whether someone has eaten.
Before you...Songs were just songs.
After you...Every melody started looking for your name.
Before you...Rain meant bad weather.
After you...It became another excuse to think about you.
Before you...I walked through places without noticing them.
After you...Every road asked me if you'd walked there too.
Before you...Home had an address.
After you...Home answered to your name.
Before you...I believed people changed because of time.
After you...I realized sometimes they change because of one person.
Before you...I never understood why people reread old chats.
After you...I found myself searching for moments I already knew by heart.
Before you...I thought forever meant never ending.
After you...I learned forever can exist inside a single memory.
Before you...I never noticed someone's typing speed.
After you...I could tell your mood before you finished your sentence.
Before you...Goodnight was the end of a conversation.
After you...It became another way of saying, "Please stay a little longer."
Before you...I never feared ordinary days.
After you...I feared the day they'd become memories.
Before you...I thought healing came with answers.
After you...I discovered it sometimes comes from one person quietly saying, "I'm here."
Before you...I was living my life.
After you...I started imagining you in every future I dreamed about.
Before you...I thought people leave memories behind.
After you...I realized some people don't leave memories. They leave a different version of you.
