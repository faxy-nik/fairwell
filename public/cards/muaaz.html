Muaaz

Kam ka bnda to ab mila hai

It started with a random coding conversation. Two people who shared a frustration and a quiet ambition. What I did not expect was that a technical discussion would open the door to one of the most honest friendships I have ever had. You are the kind of person who makes you re-evaluate what it means to connect.

Hours of talking about human nature, psychology, relationships, random theories that neither of us could let go of. The hostel talks that stretched into the early morning. The cafe gossip that somehow always circled back to something meaningful. You were not just someone to talk to. You were someone to think with.

Group chaos never felt the same without you. The laughter, the debates, the moments of quiet understanding in the middle of noise. You brought a sharpness to every conversation. A willingness to go where most people would not. And underneath it all, a brotherhood that started with a joke about being capable and became something far more real.

Kam ka bnda to ab mila hai. And I mean that in every way possible. You are a person of substance in a world that often settles for less. Thank you for the mind, the heart, and the brotherhood. This is not the end of our conversations. It is only a pause.

— Fahad Me And 8 Others
