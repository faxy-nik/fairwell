For Eeshah

The Poet
Some people arrive like sunrise —
soft, golden, impossible to ignore.

You are that kind of dawn.

تم خود کو نہیں جانتے
پر میں جانتا ہوں

Keep finding magic in the ordinary.
Keep writing the words only you can write.

And when the world feels heavy,
remember —
you carry moonlight in your voice.
✦ ✦ ✦
— Always, with love
