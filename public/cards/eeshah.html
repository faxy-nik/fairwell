You calm my chaos and somehow match it too

It started with a WhatsApp reply about Pakistan vs India. I do not remember who commented first or what exactly was said, but I remember the feeling of realising there was someone who could go there. Deeper than cricket. Deeper than small talk. That message was the first thread of something neither of us could have planned.

You are the strangest, most beautiful paradox I have ever met. You calm my chaos and somehow match it too. You are grounding without being boring, exciting without being exhausting. Being around you feels like standing in a room where the light shifts constantly — warm, surprising, never dull. You made me feel seen in a way that is rare.

20th April at Shalimar Bagh. Some days mark themselves without asking permission. That one did. It was not grand or loud. It was just true. A moment frozen in the middle of ordinary life, the kind you replay not because anything happened, but because everything did.

This is not a goodbye. It is a page turn. The story is not ending. The chapter is simply shifting. And I am grateful for every word, every silence, every laugh that filled these pages. You will always be someone I think of when I remember what it felt like to truly connect.

— Fahad Me And 8 Others
