Eeshah

You calm my chaos and somehow match it too

Some people become memories. Some people become lessons. And then there are a few rare people who become a part of who you are. You are one of those people.

If someone asked me where this chapter really started, I would probably say it was just a normal day. A day where I was helping with presentations, where you were there with Muneeba, Maham, and Aimal. Nothing dramatic. Nothing that felt like the beginning of something important. My first thought was simple. "Okay, some girl." Just a normal introduction. Just a normal conversation. A simple DM about presentations.

But life has a strange way of hiding the most meaningful stories inside the most ordinary moments. That one small conversation slowly became something else. A message became a conversation. A conversation became comfort. Comfort became trust. Trust became a place where both of us could remove the masks we usually wear around the world.

Then came music. Songs became little pieces of ourselves that we shared. Then came the calls. The long calls where hours disappeared without us even noticing. Talking about random things. Talking about life. Talking about fears. Talking about the things we usually keep locked inside.

Somewhere between those late nights and endless conversations, you stopped being just a person I knew. You became someone I looked forward to. Someone whose presence felt different. Someone whose happiness started mattering to me.

And the most beautiful part is that it was never just about the big moments. It was the tiny things. Your smile. Your eyes. Your glasses. Your voice. Your clingy little moments. Your short anger. The way you cared. The way you noticed when something was wrong. The way you would ask "what happened?" again and again until I finally stopped pretending everything was fine. Those small things were never small to me. They were pieces of you. Pieces that made you, you.

One thing I always wished for you was that you could see yourself the way I saw you. Because sometimes you forgot your own worth. You doubted yourself. You wondered if you were enough. But I hope you know that the person who questioned her own value was the same person who gave someone else comfort. The person who thought she was not enough became someone else's safe place. You were strong in ways you never gave yourself credit for. You were caring in ways you never noticed. You were beautiful in ways that had nothing to do with appearance. You were simply Eeshah. And that alone was something special.

You changed me. Not just for a moment. Not just during the time we shared. You changed the way I understand people. Before you, I kept more things inside. I carried feelings quietly. I struggled to open up. But with you, I learned that vulnerability is not something to fear. I learned that caring deeply for someone is a beautiful thing. I learned patience. I learned softness. I learned that sometimes a person can heal parts of you without even trying.

The word "Paradox" was never just a word after you. It became a reminder. Because somehow you were exactly that. You were different from everything I expected. You brought calm to chaos. You understood silence but also matched the madness. You became a contradiction that somehow made perfect sense.

Not every person is meant to stay in the exact way we imagine. But some people leave a mark that never really disappears. I don't want this chapter to only be remembered because of what happened. I want it to be remembered because of what it created. A better version of me. A version that learned how to care more. A version that learned how to love gently. A version that learned the value of another person's heart.

So Eeshah, if you ever look back at these words one day, I hope you don't focus on endings. I hope you focus on the fact that for a certain time, two people created something meaningful. Something real. Something that mattered.

I will always want you to win in life. Even if I'm not standing beside you for every step. I will always hope you find happiness. I will always hope you find peace. I will always hope you look at yourself with the kindness you gave to others.

And if one day you forget everything else — forget the small details, forget the conversations, forget the timeline — just remember this: You were loved. You were appreciated. You were never a burden. You were enough. Without even realizing it, you changed someone's life forever.

— Fahad Me And 8 Others
