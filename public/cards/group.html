📅 Our Journey

2025 — The Beginning
We walked into that classroom as strangers. Little did we know, we were about to meet some of the most important people in our lives(not really though but still).
Late Nights, Deep Talks
Shared projects turned into shared meals. Study sessions turned into 3 AM conversations about life, dreams, and everything in between.
The Inside Jokes
The memes, the nicknames, the moments that made us cry with laughter those are the threads that wove us together.
Growing Together
Through highs and lows, celebrations and struggles, we were there for each other. That's what made this group different.
2026 — Forever
As we go our separate ways, we carry a piece of each other. This isn't goodbye it's see you later. Though we fought a lot of times, there were many misunderstandings, but we always came back stronger. We are a family, and nothing can change that. There were very few moments when we didn't support each other, and that's what made us strong. At the end its just one thing that, when we meet after 2-3 months, we will be like we never left each other. We will not be the same as we were before. We will not be the same group of friends, we will evolve , emotionally mentally and spirtually cz we are together and need to be together and nothing can change that.
