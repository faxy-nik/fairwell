Opening Poem

We met in the spaces where orientation went blur,
An extrovert’s echo, a quiet girl’s stir.
But the rooftop held secrets, the night call held peace,
And the distance between us was destined to cease.
You looked for a shelter, I looked for a home,
And we found in each other what we sought all alone.

You are my Paradox, my favorite contradiction,
A beautiful truth in a world full of fiction.
Half light and half shadow, too loud in your head,
Yet you sat in my chaos where others had fled.
You did not try to fix me, nor did I ask you to change,
We just sat in the dark where the silence felt strange
And suddenly, the war in my chest felt so light,
Like a storm finding peace in the middle of night.

I remember the slope where the garden grew deep,
The bench where my arms had a promise to keep.
When I reached for your glasses, you whispered so sweet,
"Chashma pehle, aap baad," as our two worlds would meet.
And then came the kiss not of hurry or rush,
But a soft, breathing world in a heart-stopping hush.
The taste of your lips and the warmth of your hand,
A map of a country only we understand.

You told me my eyes held a depth in the sun,
Holding warm, messy thoughts where the gold rays would run.
And I fell for your eyes, and the scent of your hair,
The tantrums, the laughter, the way that you care.
I am here, my bhondu, my rasamalai, my Ash,
To rebuild the colors that the world tried to clash.
I want you to love all the pink once again,
To stand tall and heal from the scars and the pain.

I carried the weight of a world on my own,
A shelter for many, yet bleeding alone.
But the world doesn't know how the caretaker breaks,
Or the quiet, cold storms that a silent heart makes.
I wanted that breakdown with you, only you,
To show you the one tear I never let through.
For Sukuun is a person found out of the blue,
And I found my Sukuun when my heart anchored you.

We are two slots on one ship, sailing the deep,
A dream that we woke to and promised to keep.
You are me, I am you, in the dark and the shine,
Two threads interlinked by a design so divine.
So just stay, my Eeshah, let the heavy parts fall
With you by my side, we will conquer it all.
