Maham

The language without words

It started with the simplest question: "Class khatam hogyi k nahi?" Four words that somehow turned into one of the most unexpected friendships of my life. You did not enter with a grand introduction. You just appeared, and suddenly you were part of everything.

There was the funny confusion of being mixed up with Aimal. A running joke that never quite got old. But underneath the laughter was something deeper. A connection that did not need to be explained or justified. It just existed. And we both felt it.

Eye contact that replaced words. That is rare. Most people need to fill silence. With you, silence was never empty. A single glance could carry an entire conversation. And your dark humour — the kind that most people would not understand — only you got it. Only you responded in the same language.

We did not plan to become family. It just happened. Slowly, quietly, without announcement. You became someone I think of when I think of what it means to belong without trying. No labels. No expectations. Just a bond that speaks in glances and laughter and the comfort of knowing you are understood without saying a word.

— Fahad Me And 8 Others
