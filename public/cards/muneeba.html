Some chapters are not meant to be forgotten

You were the first friend I made at university. I still remember the uncertainty of walking into a room full of strangers, and then there was a WhatsApp notification. A reply. A conversation that turned into the kind of friendship that does not ask for permission. It just happens. And you were at the centre of it.

The Big Apple 500ml became our thing. A trivial detail to anyone else, but for us it was a marker of something real. The hours of music we shared, the voice notes that carried more than words ever could — these were the small acts of care that quietly added up to something enormous. You listened. You showed up. You made the ordinary feel significant.

Friendship is not built in grand gestures. It is built in the moments when someone chooses to be present. You chose that over and over again. In a city that can feel overwhelming, you were a constant. A safe place. A voice that reminded me that some connections do not need a reason.

Some chapters are not meant to be forgotten. This one is carved into the pages I will carry with me wherever I go. Thank you for being the first hand that reached out, for the laughter, for the music, for the care that never demanded anything in return.

— Fahad Me And 8 Others
