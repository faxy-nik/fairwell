The person I could talk to about anything

There are very few people you meet in life with whom the conversation never truly ends. You are one of them. We did not need daily messages or constant check-ins. What we had was something quieter and far more valuable. The knowledge that when we spoke, it would be real.

Philosophy, trust, psychology, love. We talked about all of it. The things most people tiptoe around, we walked straight into. You have a mind that does not settle for surface-level anything. And a heart that lets that mind be honest without being cold. That combination is rarer than you know.

The kind of honesty we shared is the kind most people cannot handle. Full transparency. No performance. No fear of judgment. I could say anything to you and trust that you would meet it with understanding, not reaction. That is a gift I do not take lightly.

Our friendship was not built on frequency. It was built on the unspoken agreement that time and distance do not change what is real. When we meet again, nothing will have shifted. The conversation will pick up exactly where it left off. And we will both know, without saying it, that some bonds do not need maintenance. They just are.
